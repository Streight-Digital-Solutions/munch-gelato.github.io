Switch Ringer ON for sound!

HOW TO PLAY:
  1. On your turn, roll the dice and place the corresponding Tetromino piece onto the base/tower.
  2. You cannot start with the I-Piece.
  3. WILD (?): Choose ANY Tetromimo piece to play!
  4. If all the pieces are in the tower, remove a piece from the tower and place it on top.
  5. If the tower topples on your turn, you lose!

Loser buys the next round of gelato!
