About Us

We are a small, family owned business located in the Harbour Quay in beautiful Port Alberni, BC.

We opened in July of 2025 with a vision of creating and serving unique flavours of gelato to our community, and visitors to our community.

We make our gelato from scratch using between 6-10 ingredients that you can actually pronounce! We don’t add any artificial flavours or colours to our gelato.
